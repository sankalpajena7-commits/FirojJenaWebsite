2020 – 2025

Five years of focused expansion.

The last five years under the leadership of our CEO, Firoj Jena, have been a phase of focused expansion and structured growth. Our journey moved strongly into hospitals, industrial facilities, commercial corporate fit-outs, and data centers — sectors that demand high compliance, precision engineering, and disciplined execution.

This transformation was not just about delivering projects with personal involvement at critical stages. It was about creating sustainable capability within the organisation — establishing dedicated verticals, each equipped with specialised teams capable of independently delivering complex projects in their respective domains.
