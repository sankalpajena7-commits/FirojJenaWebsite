Firoj Jena is a well-known Building Services (MEPF) Engineering Consultant with over 20 years of professional experience in the planning, design and execution of building services engineering for more than 2,000 projects, covering over 35 crore sq. ft. of built-up area across India.

Together with a multidisciplinary team of nearly 200 architects and engineers, he has successfully delivered projects across more than 90 cities in India — earning the trust of leading developers, institutions, healthcare organisations and architectural practices along the way.

Approach & Philosophy

Engineering excellence meets cost discipline.

He strongly believes that exceptional engineering is built through teamwork, ownership and attention to detail. Every assignment is approached with the same commitment and enthusiasm as the first project — resulting in long-term client relationships, with nearly 80% of the firm's business generated through repeat clients.

His approach combines engineering excellence with project cost optimisation, ensuring technically robust yet commercially viable solutions — expertise that has also contributed significantly to successful Project Management Consultancy (PMC) assignments.

A firm believer in lifelong learning, he continuously upgrades his knowledge through advanced professional courses, certifications and research — with academic interests in Construction Management and process-oriented project delivery, alongside ongoing research and technical publications in the field.
